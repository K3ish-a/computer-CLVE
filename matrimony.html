the state of two people being married and the lifelong union between two people. It is also when spouses make vows to stay and love each other for the rest of their lives. The two people must stay and not betray one another because it is a sin to commit any other type of adultery or romantic connection with another person while already being a married man/woman.

₊˚⊹ ᰔMeaning & celebration₊˚⊹ ᰔ

Matrimony is the covenant by which a baptized man and woman establish a lifelong partnership ordered toward the good of the spouses and the family.

  • Key sign: Marriage vows
  • Effect: The sacrament strengthens spouses to live faithful, loving, and fruitful Christian family life.
The sacraments are encounters with Christ that draw the Church into a life of faith, worship, and mission.

Why it matters: This sacrament is not merely a ceremony; it points to God’s action and invites the Christian community to respond with faith and love.
