Sacrament of Healing

✦•┈๑⋅⋯Reconciliation⋯⋅๑┈•✦

It is when us people confess our sins and repent to ask for God's forgiveness. When we confess our sins to the priest and to God, all of our mistakes will be forgiven even if we forget or intentionally hide some of it because God will always forgive us no matter how big the sin that we have committed.

₊˚⊹ ᰔMeaning & celebration₊˚⊹ ᰔ

In Reconciliation, a person turns back to God, confesses sins, receives absolution, and experiences the healing mercy of Christ through the ministry of the Church.

  • Key sign: Contrition and absolution
  • Effect: The sacrament restores communion with God and the Church and encourages conversion and a renewed life.
The sacraments are encounters with Christ that draw the Church into a life of faith, worship, and mission.

Why it matters: This sacrament is not merely a ceremony; it points to God’s action and invites the Christian community to respond with faith and love.
