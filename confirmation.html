SACRAMENT OF INITIATION

Strengthened
by the Spirit

Confirmation is a Sacrament of Initiation that is often described the 'the perfection of Baptism". Through the renewal of vows, the laying on of hands, and the anointing with Chrism, Christians renew and reaffirm their faith while receiving a special outpouring of the Holy Spirit.

Confirmation calls Christians to become stronger witnesses of Christ and to participate more actively in the mission of the Church.

The Holy Spirit strengthens us to live, defend, and share our faith.

UNDERSTANDING CONFIRMATION

What happens in Confirmation?

Holy Spirit

Through the sacrament of confirmation, a Christian receives a deeper union with God the Father and Jesus Christ, the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit to guide and watch over moral actions, special strength to publicly spread and defend their faith and to never be ashamed of the cross.

Sacred Chrism

Sacred chrism, a blessed oil, is used during the anointing of the person being confirmed.

Gifts of the Spirit

Confirmation deepens our baptismal life and relationship by God as it seals the Holy Spirit within us — blessing us with its 7 gifts. This sacrament also helps us to confidently live out, defend, and spread our faith.

Signs and Symbols♡

Chrism Oil — symbolizes being sealed and strengthened by the Holy Spirit.

Dove — represents the Holy Spirit.

Fire — symbolizes the power and presence of the Holy Spirit.

Laying on of Hands — expresses the invocation of the Holy Spirit.

WHY IT MATTERS

Living as a Witness

Confirmation connects to the Christian life as it is the sacrament that completes the grace of baptism. The believer is sealed with the Holy Spirit and blessed with its 7 gifts, and is given the strength to be an active and faithful member of the Church and witness of Christ.
