Seven Sacraments

The Sacraments are sacred signs instituted by Christ and entrusted to the Church. Through them, God gives us His grace and helps us grow in our relationship with Him. The Catholic Church celebrates Seven Sacraments, which accompany us Christians throughout important stages of our life.
